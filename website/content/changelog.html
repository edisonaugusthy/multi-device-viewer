Upcoming — Floating device previews

Click Open floating preview in the workspace toolbar, then search for a device or pick one from the list. The preview opens over your page so you can keep using the original tab. Drag the frame to move it. Hover over the device to reveal a small tab above it with a close button, and drag the bracket at its lower-left corner to resize it.

Upcoming — Pop Out, Picture-in-Picture, and guest sessions

  • Pop Out: Click Open floating preview in a device's toolbar to move that device over the current page.
  • Picture-in-Picture: Click Keep on top of other windows in a device's toolbar to keep the live preview above your editor and other apps. The window shows just the device and keeps its website session. You can also choose On top of other apps in the floating preview picker, use Add device on top of other apps in the right-click menu, or click the window button above a floating device. Closing the window closes the device. Available on HTTPS and localhost in Chrome.
  • Feature tour: Pop Out and Picture-in-Picture have their own callouts in the first-run tour. Returning users see the new steps after closing What's New.
  • Easier floating controls: The tab above a floating device stays long enough to reach from any side.
  • Guest session: Turn on Guest session in a device's options to see the page signed out on that device, next to devices that stay signed in. You can also sign in there as a second user without affecting your own login.
